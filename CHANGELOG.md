# Changelog

## v1.2 — 2026-11-10

- **Memorial ("IN MEMORIAM" pages in the SELECT records):** every fallen Pokémon with icon, nickname, level, where it died and what killed it ("Fell to Falkner's PIDGEOTTO", "Fell to a wild GEODUDE", "Died to poison", "Buried by the sandstorm", recoil, self-KO moves, Destiny Bond and more). Also shown on the run-over screen.
- **Saving panel:** the autosave after a battle now shows what happened (catch, lost encounter, deaths with cause, victory) while the game saves, then "GAME SAVED".
- **Area status popup:** entering an area with wild Pokémon shows "First encounter available!" or "Encounter used: PIDGEY ○/×" next to the place-name sign.
- **Encounter log extras:** shiny-clause catches, gifts (starter, Eevee, Game Corner prizes...), hatched Eggs and one-time encounters (legendaries, Sudowoodo, roamers...) appear as gold bonus entries.
- **Stricter nicknames:** blank names are refused, and gift / hatched Pokémon always get the naming screen.
- Professor Oak's speech mentions nicknaming received Pokémon and the memorial.

## v1.1 — 2026-11-10

- **Faster play:** autosave only happens after battles that matter (a death, a catch, an area's first encounter, beating Lance or Red), instead of before and after every encounter. This removes the long pauses on Delta.
- Professor Oak's rules speech updated to match ("the game saves whenever it matters").

## v1.0 — 2026-11-10

First release.

- Core Nuzlocke rules: encounter rule, shiny clause, permadeath with GRAVEYARD box, forced nicknames, wipeout = game over, autosave / no soft reset.
- Professor Oak explains the rules in the intro.
- NUZLOCKE RUN OVER screen with final team; a finished save always reopens on it.
- Victory screens for Champion Lance and Red, saved as snapshots.
- Records screen on SELECT: current run, encounter log, victories.
- Encounter log: first encounter per area with ○ caught / × failed.
- Pokégear map Nuzlocke view (SELECT on the map).
- Test mode (hold L/R + SELECT for 5 s) that never writes to the save file.
